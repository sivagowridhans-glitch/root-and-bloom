Our approach to medical information

Every piece of health-related content in the app (such as the warning-sign explanations for anemia, high blood pressure, and gestational diabetes) is written in plain language based on publicly available maternal-health guidance, and is intended purely for general awareness.

Root & Bloom does not diagnose, treat, or replace a doctor's advice. We actively encourage every user to discuss any symptom, concern, or decision with their own obstetrician or hospital care team.

Working with hospitals

We're keen to partner with hospitals and clinics to make this a genuinely useful companion for your patients between visits — whether that's a QR code in your antenatal clinic, or feedback from your medical team to improve the app's content.

If your hospital would like to review the app, suggest changes to the medical content, or discuss a partnership, we'd love to hear from you.
