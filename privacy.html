Your pregnancy and health information is sensitive, and we want you to know exactly what we do — and don't do — with it.

What we collect

When you use Root & Bloom, we may store the following on your device and/or our servers:

  • Your last menstrual period (LMP) date and calculated pregnancy week
  • Daily mood check-ins and symptom notes you choose to enter
  • Kick-counter sessions and water-intake logs
  • Hospital-bag checklist progress
  • Questions you type into the "Ask anything" assistant
  • Payment confirmation (not card details) if you unlock Premium via Razorpay

How we use it

Your data is used only to power the features you see in the app — for example, showing your week progress, your mood history, or your checklist status. We do not sell your personal or health data to advertisers or third parties.

Third-party services

We use Razorpay to process Premium payments; Razorpay handles your payment details directly under its own privacy policy. If a hospital partner shares this app with you, the hospital does not automatically receive your in-app data unless you explicitly choose to share it (e.g. by showing a hospital staff member your app).

Your rights

Medical disclaimer
